The requested outcome is to return an article's complete primary PMC asset answer without spending the optional Europe PMC ZIP budget. Missing, failed or unusable primary data must still reach the existing fallback sources and preserve their refusal and unavailable labels. This work starts at 63e786985, which adds the ticket to main 3386. The cause is the unconditional ZIP member of the assets resolver's tokio::join!: a complete primary reply still waits for that optional future.
