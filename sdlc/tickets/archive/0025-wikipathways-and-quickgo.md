# Add WikiPathways as third pathway source

BioMCP's pathway entity currently federates KEGG and Reactome. WikiPathways is the third major community-curated pathway database with a clean free JSON API, no auth required, and good coverage of disease-specific and signaling pathways that complement the other two sources. Adding it rounds out the pathway story for the paper ("three pathway databases, one command") and was validated live in experiment 032.
