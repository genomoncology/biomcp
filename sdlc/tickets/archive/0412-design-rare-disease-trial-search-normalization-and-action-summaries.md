# Design rare-disease trial search normalization and action summaries

A real rare-disease trial-search workflow exposed a gap in BioMCP's normalization and ClinicalTrials.gov result surface. The user had a child with autism / neurodevelopmental disorder and genetic testing consistent with Phelan-McDermid syndrome caused by a pathogenic SHANK3 abnormality. The practical question was whether there were active clinical trials for SHANK3-related Phelan-McDermid syndrome, including cases caused by SHANK3 sequence variants or 22q13.3 chromosomal deletion involving SHANK3, and whether University of Michigan / Michigan was a current or pending site.
