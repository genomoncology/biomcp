# Add WHO Prequalification as global regulatory region

BioMCP has FDA (US) and EMA (EU) drug regulatory data. WHO Prequalification determines drug access for billions of patients across 100+ countries — whether a cancer drug is WHO-prequalified determines if LMIC patients can access it through procurement agencies. Adding WHO PQ as a third region completes the global regulatory picture.
