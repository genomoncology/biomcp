# Experiment: biomedical concept discovery layer

BioMCP requires users to know what entity type they want (`get gene`, `search drug`, `search trial`). A discovery layer would resolve general keywords like "chest pain", "Keytruda", or "BRCA testing" into structured biomedical concepts and route users to the right BioMCP commands. This experiment validates three candidate APIs (EBI OLS4, UMLS Metathesaurus, MedlinePlus) to determine which could serve as a "front door" for BioMCP.
