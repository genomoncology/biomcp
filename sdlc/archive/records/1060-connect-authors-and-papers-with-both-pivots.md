# Connect authors and papers with both pivots

BioMCP now pivots from exact Semantic Scholar authors to paginated compact papers and from accepted article IDs to provider-qualified bylines. Author detail links to the paper pivot, while both paths preserve evidence, follow-up commands, and the existing privacy boundary.
