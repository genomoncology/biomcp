BioMCP now requests ClinicalTrials.gov stop reasons and shows them beside
terminated, withdrawn, or suspended statuses in Markdown and JSON. Checked
absence remains visible, while ordinary trial output keeps its prior shape.
This lets readers distinguish why studies stopped instead of relying on status
alone.
